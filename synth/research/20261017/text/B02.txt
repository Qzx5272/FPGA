49/61-key
gesture sensitive keybed
An expressive keybed like no other
Built around the custom Augmented Keyboard Action, Osmose connects every gesture - press, bend, shake or strum - to sound with uncanny responsiveness. Its extended polyphonic pressure, high-resolution aftertouch, and individual pitch control offer a playing experience closer to an acoustic instrument than a typical synth.
Watch tutorial
custom expressive sound engine
by Haken Audio
EaganMatrix is a modular synthesis engine that interprets every gesture in real time, creating a deep connection between player and sound. It offers vast sonic possibilities from analog to physical modeling. For advanced sound design, the powerful Haken Editor (Mac/PC) lets you explore and shape sounds beyond traditional modular systems.
Watch tutorial
more than 500 presets
with tailored macros
Osmose features more than 500 presets spanning a diverse sonic palette. From cinematic strings, evolving FM pianos, and powerful electro basses to dynamic percussion, lush synth pads, expressive wind instruments, and textured SFX, each sound is crafted to respond to expressive gestures. Tailored macros let you easily shape textures—unlocking endless creative possibilities.
Watch tutorial
external midi control w/
classic & mpe keyboard configurations
Osmose lets you control any soft or hardware synth via DIN MIDI and USB, unlocking their full potential. It supports polyphonic aftertouch and initial pressure, as well as per note pitch bend for deep expression. If you prefer simplicity, switch to piano mode for classic velocity control. Configure all MIDI settings easily on Osmose’s built-in screen, no computer needed.
Watch tutorial
large color screen
& ergonomic UI
Osmose features a large color screen and a custom operating system designed for a playful and intuitive experience. No endless menus or shift buttons, just direct access to more than 500 expressive presets, smart categories, and powerful macro controls. Tweak sounds easily, explore gestures freely, and enjoy a fun and rewarding learning curve from the very first note.

built-in mpe arpeggiator
& innovative glide control
Osmose includes a built-in MPE arpeggiator and an innovative glide control for new ways of playing. Create intricate rhythmic patterns that respond to your gestures and explore fluid pitch transitions with intuitive key movements. These features offer expressive tools to expand your musical language, whether you’re crafting cinematic textures or experimental grooves.

3-axis motions
for every key
A unique mechanism captures three-dimensional gestures on every key. Extended pressure shapes timbre and volume from the first touch. High-resolution aftertouch reveals new layers as you press deeper, while lateral motion enables per-note bends and vibrato, just like on an acoustic instrument.

technical specs
keybed
49 or 61 full-size keys with three-dimensional control
configurations
standalone synthesizer, MPE controller, and classic MIDI controller
gestural control
Tap, Press, Pitch Bend, Vibrato, Aftertouch, Pressure Glide, Note Off, expressive arpeggiator
built-in effects
1 global reverb/delay/echo effect, 1 compressor/drive, 1 shelve EQ. More effects programmable via the EaganMatrix
sound engine
EaganMatrix, a digital modular engine by Haken Audio
polyphony
up to 24 voices
interface
color LCD screen, pitch, and modulation sliders
preset management
580 factory presets, up to 2048 preset slots
pedal inputs
2 continuous pedal inputs, assignable to sustain or synth parameters
midi
DIN MIDI In, MIDI Out/Thru, USB Type B
audio output
two ¼" TRS pseudo-balanced line outputs,
¼" TRS headphone output
dimensions
Model 49 keys: 90 (H) x 310 (L) x 895 (l) mm
/ 3.5” (H) x 12.2” (L) x 35.2” (l)
Model 61 keys: 90 (H) x 310 (L) x 1060 (l) mm
/ 3.5” (H) x 12.2” (L) x 41.7” (l)
weight
Model 49 keys: 8.3 kg / 18.4 lbs
Model 61 keys: 9.5 kg / 20.9 lbs
power
external PSU with lockable connector, 12V, 1.5A, center positive
software
OsmoseUpdater: application that installs new firmware and library versions, requires Windows 10+ or macOS 11+
Haken Editor: Mac/PC editor to create and edit sounds in-depth; requires installation of free Max MSP by Cycling '74
