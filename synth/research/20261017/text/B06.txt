Frequency Asked Questions (FAQ)
What is Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro, and how is it different from other AI singing or vocal generator software?
Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro is a professional AI vocal synthesis software designed for music producers, composers, songwriters, sound designers, and vocalists. Unlike simple AI song generators, it provides professional-grade control over tone, dynamics, and expression using licensed recordings of real singers. This ensures a high-quality, ethically sourced vocal synthesis experience suitable for commercial production.
How do I get started with Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro?
Download the Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro installer
here
. After installation, launch the software and select a voice – an AI singing voice model trained from professionally recorded performances created specifically for training – to begin creating vocals. The free trial version includes all core features, allowing you to test the workflow before purchasing a license.
What is a voice, and how are Synthesizer V voices made?
A voice is a professionally recorded and AI-trained voice library created from real singers. Each voice captures the singer’s unique tone, range, and expressive characteristics. Dreamtonics voices are recorded under commercial licensing agreements and trained using proprietary AI vocal synthesis models.
Can I use Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro and its voices for commercial projects?
Yes. Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro and all official Dreamtonics voices include commercial use licenses, allowing you to release, distribute, or monetize songs created with them. Review each voice’s license terms for specific details on professional and promotional use.
How does Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro achieve realistic singing performance?
The software uses advanced digital signal processing algorithms and deep neural networks (what people now refer to as “AI”) to model expressive singing based on real human performances. Users can edit pitch curves, vibrato depth, and phoneme timing through an intuitive piano-roll interface — achieving professional-grade results for studio recording, music production, song writing, learning vocals, and sound design.
Do I need a subscription to use Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro?
No. Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro is sold as a one-time paid license. There are no recurring monthly subscription fees. Once purchased, you own the software permanently and receive free updates within the same major version (e.g. 2.1.x, 2.2.x, …). You will never be automatically charged after your trial ends.
Can I try Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro and its voices for free, and do I need to cancel afterwards?
You can explore nearly all features of Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro through the free trial version—no credit card required and no automatic renewals or charges. When the trial period ends, the software simply reverts to limited functionality, so there’s nothing to unsubscribe or cancel.
Each voice also includes a 7-day free trial, allowing you to test the singer’s tone and language performance in your projects. The voice trial ends automatically after seven days, and you will not be charged or enrolled in any subscription plan. You can purchase the full version at any time inside the Product Manager to continue using the voice.
What languages and voices does Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro support?
Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro supports multilingual voices, including English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin Chinese, and Cantonese, with more languages planned. Each voice is developed in collaboration with professional singers to ensure natural tone, accurate pronunciation, and expressive performance in its native language.
What DAWs and plugin formats does Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro support?
Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro integrates with most major digital audio workstations (DAWs) and is available in the following plugin formats:
VST3 Instrument
AU Instrument

The VST3 and AU versions have been verified to work with the following DAWs:

The ARA version has been confirmed to function with:

Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro can also run as a standalone AI vocal synthesis application for independent use.
Can Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro run offline, and does it send my data to any servers?
Yes. Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro performs AI vocal synthesis locally on your computer, and once activated with a purchased voice, it does not upload your creative content to servers for processing. This local processing architecture ensures faster performance, data privacy, and full control over your creative work—ideal for professional studios and production environments with strict security policies.
When using trial voices, your data may be sent to Dreamtonics servers for license verification and processing, and offline mode is not available. Once a voice is purchased and activated, all vocal processing is done locally.
What are Dreamtonics’ principles on AI ethics and data licensing?
Dreamtonics is committed to developing ethical AI voice synthesis technology. All voices are created using licensed recordings from professional singers who have given informed consent. We ensure that artists are properly credited and compensated, and we strictly prohibit the use of unauthorized voice data or cloning without consent.
Does Dreamtonics offer volume or educational licenses for Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro?
Yes. Dreamtonics provides volume licensing options for organizations, studios, and educational institutions that require multiple seats or installations. Educational licenses are available for qualified schools, teachers, and students at a
